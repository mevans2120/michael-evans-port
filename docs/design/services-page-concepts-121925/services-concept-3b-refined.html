The best partnerships start with good questions

Here are the ones I hear most often from teams considering working together.

Sanity CMS fields for each exchange:
- question: text
- questionLabel: string (optional, default "Common question")
- serviceTitle: string
- answerParagraphs: array of text blocks

"We have a vision, but no clear path. Can you help?"

Common question

Product Strategy

Absolutely. I work with teams to translate ambition into actionable plans. We'll clarify your vision, validate market opportunities, and create a roadmap that aligns your team and stakeholders.

This usually means deep discovery, competitive analysis, and helping you prioritize ruthlessly so you can ship what matters most.

"Everyone's talking about AI. Where do we even start?"

Common question

AI/ML Implementation

Start with the business problem, not the technology. I help teams identify where AI can genuinely add value - then build practical solutions that actually ship to production.

This isn't about chasing trends. It's about finding the right application of AI that drives measurable business outcomes.

"Our product works, but users struggle with it."

Common question

Experience Design

That gap between "works" and "delights" is where I focus. I'll dig into user behavior, identify friction points, and redesign the experience to match how people actually think and work.

Good UX isn't decoration - it's the difference between a product people tolerate and one they recommend.

"Engineering and product aren't aligned."

Common question

Technical Leadership

This is often a communication and process problem, not a people problem. I embed with teams to improve how decisions get made, how work gets prioritized, and how progress gets measured.

The goal is sustainable velocity - shipping consistently without burning out your team.
