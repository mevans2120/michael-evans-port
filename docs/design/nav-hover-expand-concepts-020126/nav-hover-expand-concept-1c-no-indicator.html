Concept 1C: No Indicator

250ms delay with zero visual feedback during the wait. The nav just expands after hovering - pure and minimal.

  • No visual changes during delay
  • Shortest delay (250ms) compensates for lack of feedback
  • Feels responsive, almost instant
  • Relies on user learning the timing naturally

This is the macOS Dock approach - no indicator, just a tuned delay.

Variant C: Pure delay, no indicator. Relies entirely on timing feel. Cleanest visually but requires users to learn the behavior.
