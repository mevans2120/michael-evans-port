Concept 3: Typewriter Reveal

Warm • Human • Editorial

Design Rationale

A warm, human-centered loading state inspired by editorial design and vintage typewriters. Uses earthy tones and a blinking cursor to evoke the feeling of thoughtful composition in progress.

Key Features:

  • Blinking cursor animation (1s on/off cycle) for familiar typewriter feel
  • Warm earth tones: olive green (#2D5016) and golden (#8B6914) accents
  • Monospace font (IBM Plex Mono) for technical-yet-warm aesthetic
  • Progress indicator dots below for visual feedback on process
  • Light mode design breaks from typical dark chat UIs

Best For: Users who want a more approachable, human-feeling AI experience. The typewriter metaphor suggests careful thought and composition, making the wait feel purposeful rather than technical.
